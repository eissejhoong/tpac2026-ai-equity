Meet Maria.

She’s blind. She points her phone at her medicine box, and AI reads the label out loud.

For her, AI means independence.

Examples that already work

So what helps?

Accessible by default

Build accessibility into shared design systems, so every team gets it for free.

Shared, open tools

Free, open-source accessibility checkers help any team catch barriers early.

People in the room

People with disabilities test and co-design. In paired testing, a tester with a disability works with an abled tester.

Defences against scams

Scam-blocking apps flag suspicious calls and texts. A family “safe word” beats a cloned voice.
